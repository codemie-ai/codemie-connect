# Reply (step 2) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The reference app's reply rules in both views: one Send button named for its target, a ✦ draft link and a "✦ Tell AI what to change" line instead of the options popover, Teams sending on the user's click, a short To/Cc confirm for Outlook, ⌘↩ armed only by the user's own touch, and at most three visible controls on an open item (primary named action, Snooze ▾, ⋯).

**Architecture:** Native sending keeps its two-step, content-bound path (`action_prepare` then `action_confirm`, both gated on `mail_send` / `chat_send`). The UI chains the two calls in one click for Teams and puts a short confirm between them for Outlook. Native gains two fields: `newRecipients` in the mail review, and an optional `revise` text on `ai_draft`. A pure `reply-model.js` decides the primary action, its label, the confirm lines and the ⌘↩ gate. One `replyForm` and one `replyBar` in `view-inbox.js` serve the Default reader and the Compact open row.

**Tech Stack:** Rust (Tauri v2), `cargo test` with `FakeRunner`; vanilla JS ES modules, `node:test`; Playwright (`playwright-core` 1.63) against the mocked bridge.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-reply-design.md`. Definition of done: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-workspace-next-rollout-design.md` § "Definition of done for every step".

## Review Focus

1. A draft arrives (or the Today Draft reply pill fills the box) and the user presses ⌘↩ without touching the box: nothing is prepared or sent. After they click in the box, ⌘↩ sends once; holding it sends nothing more. (Task 5 unit tests, Task 6 R7.)
2. The mail confirm is open and the user presses Return, with focus on Cancel or after tabbing to Send: the dialog closes and `action_confirm` is never called. (Task 3 `openModal` test, Task 6 R6.)
3. A double click on "Send to Jordan", or a background sync re-rendering the row mid-send, sends one message only. (Task 5 `replyInFlight` path, Task 6 R4.)
4. Text typed and then left (row closed, route changed, view switched, window hidden) before the 1.5 s autosave is still there when the conversation opens again. (Task 4 flush, Task 6 R9.)
5. Microsoft's Reply-To points to an address that is not the sender: the confirm tags it "(new)", and a destination that changes between confirm and send is refused inline. (Task 1 pure test, Task 6 R6 with the mock's `window.__replyTo`.)
